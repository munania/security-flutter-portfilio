FIG 1.0 // PROFILE

Control is an illusion.

Who I am, how I work, and what is on record.

The world is a vulnerable system. We patch the holes, not for glory, but because the alternative is chaos. I do not sell security. I sell the absence of panic.

Most of what passes for security is theater, a performance for people who want to feel safe. I do the boring work instead: root cause analysis, threat hunting, and hardening the things that break quietly before they break loudly. Whether the actor is state sponsored or a bored teenager, the vulnerability does not care about your brand. My job is to care about it first.

My approach folds OWASP principles into web testing and secure configuration into mobile deployment, so systems keep their integrity under stress, whether that stress is an attack or ordinary load.

Outside client work, I run a self hosted Docker and Kubernetes home lab and build open source security tooling. It keeps the instincts sharp between engagements.
